The Hindi files are translations/adaptations of the enumerated reader-facing
source files. The translation changes natural-language prose, headings,
statements, proofs, exercises, captions, and navigation into Hindi while
preserving mathematical notation, LaTeX commands, labels, OLP stable IDs,
formal identifiers, and source-linked assets. Hindi locale/build support and a
pinned OFL Devanagari font are added. The cumulative wrapper adds a Hindi title
page, scope/cursor notice, DOI, table of contents, and compact running footer.

The full upstream `LICENSE.md` and the font's `OFL.txt` are included with the
editable sources and evidence. No Open Logic Project endorsement is asserted.
